Questions

Good to know

How do I use ToolsCrest Bot?

Open @Toolscrestbot, press Start and follow the prompts to upload a supported file and choose an operation.

Do I need to install another app?

No separate app is required. You use the bot through Telegram.

Which files are supported?

The current project supports PDF, JPEG, PNG and WebP inputs for relevant operations. Individual tools may have different requirements and limits.

How do paid plans work?

Open the bot and use its Plans or Buy options to check current pricing and payment availability. Do not rely on an old screenshot or third-party price listing.

Where can I get help with a payment?

Use the bot's /paysupport command for payment-related support.
